CONTRAST CAMERA

— fps
Press START CAMERA and allow camera access.

Start with “Pure black & white,” then try “Black & white + outlines.” Adjust the threshold for different lighting. This is a prototype: edges are detected, but people are not segmented from the background. Do not use for walking, driving, or other safety-critical navigation. Video stays on your device; it is not uploaded.

Requires camera permission and a secure context (HTTPS or localhost).
